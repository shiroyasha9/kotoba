How to review

Listen to the clip and type exactly what you meant to say, word for word. This text is the reference that every speech-to-text model is scored against.

  • Keep the words you spoke. Do not clean them up into nicer prose, and do not add words you did not say.
  • Keep every identifier exact: file names, commands, flags, function names and product names in their real spelling and case (auth.ts, --force, useEffect).
  • Leave out fillers such as "um" and "uh". Normal punctuation is fine; the scorer ignores it.
  • If you cannot tell what you said, or the clip has no speech, press Skip.

Keys: Space play or pause, ← → seek 2 s, Shift+← 0.5x speed, Shift+→ 1x speed, ⌘+Enter save and go to the next clip. In the text box, hold ⌥ with the audio keys, or press Esc to leave the box.
